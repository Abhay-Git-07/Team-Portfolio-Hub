OUR PURPOSE

Turning Learning into Real-World Experience

This project gives us an opportunity to apply our classroom knowledge to a practical web development project. From designing responsive interfaces to organizing code and collaborating through GitHub, every step helps us develop technical skills and a stronger understanding of teamwork.

🤝

Teamwork & Collaboration

We share responsibilities, exchange ideas, and work together to bring different parts of the project into one consistent experience.

💻

Creative Frontend Development

We use HTML5 and CSS3 to build structured, visually appealing, and responsive pages that make our team's work easy to explore.

🔀

Learning Git & GitHub
